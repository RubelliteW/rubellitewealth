About the Firm

Rubellite Wealth Management is a boutique advisory practice dedicated to the complex financial needs of incorporated business owners and high-net-worth families across Canada.

We do not manage pooled funds or serve the general public. Every relationship is deeply personal, long-term, and built around the specific legal and tax structure of each client's enterprise.

Our work sits at the intersection of corporate tax planning, estate law, and investment strategy — disciplines that must be coordinated, not siloed, to achieve lasting results.

Questions?

Frequently asked questions

What makes Rubellite different from other wealth advisors?+

We specialize exclusively in corporate estate and succession planning. Rather than general investment advice, we coordinate across tax, legal, and investment strategy for incorporated business owners. This specialized focus means deeper expertise in your specific situation.

Who is a typical Rubellite client?+

While we frequently work with incorporated business owners, professionals (such as doctors, dentists, and lawyers), high-net-worth families, and their successors to optimize substantial assets and tax strategies, we strongly believe that no client is too small. Frankly, the sooner you start planning with us, the better positioned you will be for the future. Whether you are just starting to build your wealth or are looking to manage an existing estate, we are here to help you grow.

What are your costs to get started?+

We offer a complimentary, confidential introductory consultation to discuss your situation and determine if our approach is the right fit. This typically runs 30–45 minutes and helps both parties understand alignment before entering a formal engagement.

Are you just another insurance broker?+

Not at all. We view insurance not as a standalone product, but as a strategic lever within a complete financial plan. As wealth advisors, our core focus is helping you structure, protect, and grow your overall wealth—integrating tax planning, corporate strategies, and estate design to ensure every strategy actively protects and advances your long-term goals.

What if I already have a financial plan or structure in place?+

That is no problem at all—in fact, we would be happy to audit your current setup at no cost to you. Wealth isn’t stagnant; it is constantly growing and evolving, and your planning and corporate structure should adapt right alongside it. We can provide a second set of eyes to ensure you aren't leaving any opportunities on the table.
