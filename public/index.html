About the Firm

Rubellite Wealth Management is a boutique advisory practice dedicated to the complex financial needs of incorporated business owners and high-net-worth families across Canada.

We do not manage pooled funds or serve the general public. Every relationship is deeply personal, long-term, and built around the specific legal and tax structure of each client's enterprise.

Our work sits at the intersection of corporate tax planning, estate law, and investment strategy — disciplines that must be coordinated, not siloed, to achieve lasting results.
