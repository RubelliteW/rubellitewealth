What We Do

Focus Areas

We concentrate our practice on three interconnected disciplines — each essential to a complete wealth strategy for the incorporated business owner.

Focus 01

Corporate Estate Planning

Related Practice Areas

For most incorporated business owners, the corporation itself holds the majority of accumulated wealth. Without deliberate planning, a significant portion of that wealth — sometimes more than half — can be lost to taxes at death before it reaches the next generation.

Corporate estate planning addresses this directly. We work to structure your corporate assets, insurance, and shareholder agreements so that ownership transfers at death or disability are as efficient and smooth as possible.

This includes corporately owned life insurance, estate freeze structures, capital dividend account strategies, and coordination with your will and power of attorney to ensure everything works together.

Focus 02

Business Succession Planning

Our Process

Succession planning is the discipline of deciding — and then engineering — how ownership and control of your business will transfer. Whether you intend to pass it to the next generation, sell to a partner, or exit to a third party, the financial and legal structures required are complex and time-sensitive.

We work alongside your accountant and legal counsel to ensure that shareholder agreements, buy-sell provisions, and tax-planning structures are in place long before a transition event occurs. A well-structured succession plan preserves business value and protects family relationships.

It also ensures that you, the departing owner, are financially secure regardless of how the transition unfolds — including scenarios of death, disability, or disagreement among shareholders.

Focus 03

Wealth Accumulation & Preservation

Practice Areas

Incorporated business owners accumulate wealth across multiple vehicles — retained earnings inside a corporation, registered plans (RRSP, TFSA, IPP), and personal investment portfolios. Each vehicle has a different tax treatment, different contribution rules, and different estate implications.

Our role is to coordinate these streams into a coherent, tax-efficient strategy. This includes selecting the right mix of investment vehicles, managing the timing of income extraction, and ensuring that the structure evolves as your business and family situation change.

Preservation is inseparable from accumulation. We incorporate creditor protection strategies, disability coverage, and corporate structures designed to ring-fence accumulated wealth from the operating risks of your business.

"Corporate estate planning, succession, and wealth accumulation are not separate disciplines — they are one conversation, and they must be had together."

— Rubellite Wealth Management
